      Hear it

      Press Play to hear the real bass from the recording while the marker follows along in the tab. If the marker and the sound disagree, the tab is probably wrong there.

      Why are there two sounds?

      The real bass is the bass player from the recording, separated from the other instruments. Think of it as the answer key.

      The tab played back is what this tool wrote down, played by a computer bass. If it sounds different from the real bass, the tab is wrong in that spot.

      Playing both at once lets you hear where they disagree. They may slowly drift apart, because they are two separate players.
