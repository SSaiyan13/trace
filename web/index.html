      More ways to listen

      Why are there two sounds?

      The real bass is the player from the recording, separated from the other instruments. Think of it as the answer key.

      The tab played back is what TRACE wrote down, played by a computer. If it sounds different from the real recording, the tab is wrong in that spot.

      Playing both at once lets you hear where they disagree. They may slowly drift apart, because they are two separate players.

      Marker ahead of or behind the sound?

      Wireless headphones and some speakers play the sound a little late. Nudge the marker until it lands on the notes as you hear them. TRACE remembers this on this device.
